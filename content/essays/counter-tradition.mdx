Everything else on this site describes how control works. If that is all you take, the
material has done you harm rather than good, because a person who can explain every mechanism
and act on none of them is worse off than when they started.

This is the constructive half. It is thinner than the diagnostic half, which is itself a
finding worth sitting with.

## Mills: turn a trouble into an issue

[C. Wright Mills](/figures/mills) supplies the first move and this site opens with it. A
personal failure that turns out to be a shared structural condition is a different object
entirely, and it can be acted on collectively in a way that a private failing cannot.

The move is not comfort. It is a reclassification, and it is testable: if fifteen million
people have your problem, your character is not the variable.

## La Boétie: the power is lent

[Étienne de La Boétie](/figures/la-boetie), writing around 1548, is the oldest and simplest
version. The tyrant has only what you hand over. His account of the ladder of complicity, in
which each layer polices the one below because it has a stake, is also the reason withdrawal
is hard: you are not one person deciding, you are one person in a structure of people
deciding.

## Freire: literacy as reading your own situation

Paulo Freire, *Pedagogy of the Oppressed* (1968), sets banking education, in which
information is deposited into passive receptacles, against *conscientização*, critical
consciousness built through dialogue.

Teaching adults to read, in his practice, meant teaching them to read their own situation at
the same time, using words drawn from their own conditions. It is the clearest available
alternative to the school [Bourdieu](/thinkers/bourdieu) and [Illich](/thinkers/illich)
describe.

## Gramsci: the long build

[Gramsci's](/thinkers/gramsci) war of position is the strategic version. Institutions,
publications, trained people, decades. Whatever else it is, it is not a quick answer, and
[the neoliberal thought collective](/milieus/mont-pelerin) is the twentieth century's best
evidence that it works when someone actually does it for fifty years.

Hold it against [Michels](/figures/michels), who says the organization you build will grow
its own oligarchy. That tension is unresolved and it is in the
[open questions](/open-questions).

## Illich: keep the tool below the threshold

[Illich](/thinkers/illich) is the only figure here whose work is mainly about building. A
convivial tool is one anyone can pick up for their own purposes, which enlarges what they can
do. *Tools for Conviviality* (1973) is the design brief, and the test it proposes is simple
enough to apply to anything you are about to build: does this make people more capable
without it, or less?

## Wiener: refuse the funding, in public

[Norbert Wiener](/figures/wiener) published his refusal of military funding in the *Atlantic*
in January 1947, with his reasons.

It is a precedent rather than a theory, and it did not change the field. It is on this list
because it is the only case in the whole document of someone at the centre of a technology
trying to withdraw it from the institution that paid for it, and because any project built
out of this research has to answer the same question about its own money.

## Havel: take the sign down

[Havel's](/figures/havel) living in truth is the smallest possible unit of action, and the
most demanding. Not organizing, not arguing, just declining to perform the assent. He is
clear about the cost, which is what separates it from a slogan.

## Scott: the hidden transcript is already there

[James C. Scott](/figures/scott) is the reminder that people subject to all of this are not
waiting to be told. The hidden transcript exists in every workplace and every institution.
Mētis, the practical knowledge that cannot be formalized, is still held by the people doing
the work, and defending it is a live political act rather than nostalgia.

## Bourdieu: turn it on yourself first

[Bourdieu's](/thinkers/bourdieu) reflexivity is the discipline that stops this material
turning into a way of feeling superior to other people. You are also positioned. Your taste,
your credentials and your sense of what is obvious came from somewhere.

Applied honestly, that is uncomfortable, which is how you know it is the real version.
