This material has two ways of going wrong. They look like opposites and they are
produced by the same laziness.

## Failure mode A, the sanctioned critique

Organizations funded by, and dependent on access to, the institutions they study produce
criticism that never threatens the source of the funding. The result is a steady supply
of alternatives that reinforce the original arrangement.

The leash is not censorship. It is incentive, operating through selection. Nobody is
told what to conclude. People whose conclusions are convenient get the grant, the
invitation and the next job, and they are not lying when they say they wrote what they
thought.

[The Congress for Cultural Freedom](/milieus/congress-for-cultural-freedom) is the
historical proof of concept, and it is the reason this site is careful about who pays
for what.

## Failure mode B, the theory of everything

The mirror image. These frameworks explain a great deal, which tempts the user into
explaining everything with them: one cause, a story no evidence could contradict, and a
pleasant sense of having seen through it all.

That is not the strong version of this thinking. It is the same laziness wearing black.

[Agamben on COVID](/thinkers/agamben) is the worked example, published here rather than
quietly omitted, because it is what a formidable framework looks like when it stops
discriminating.

## The four questions

Put these to every source, including this one.

1. **Who paid, and what did they want?** Foundation, military, party, industry,
   university. Follow the grant rather than the footnote. Almost every mid-century
   finding about communications and behaviour traces back to a military or foundation
   programme with an applied goal.
2. **What was the institution's actual job?** A bureau measuring radio audiences for
   advertisers produces a different theory of the public than a seminar in exile writing
   against fascism, even when the two share a building. In
   [one case](/milieus/radio-project) they literally did.
3. **What crisis was in the room?** Total war, occupation, decolonization, inflation, a
   collapsing empire. Ideas about control get written when control is visibly failing or
   visibly being built.
4. **What could not be said there?** The boundary of the sayable inside an institution is
   often the most informative thing about it.

Follow the money as routine practice rather than as an accusation. Funding shapes
findings through selection and incentive far more often than through instruction, and
the selection story is both truer and more interesting.

## The rules that follow

1. Every claim traces to a source you can check. Where this site summarizes, the source
   is named so you can go and disagree with it.
2. Every thinker page carries the strongest objections to that thinker. Never collapsed,
   never in a footnote, never softened.
3. **Structural tendency is not conspiracy.** Institutions systematically produce an
   outcome because of how incentives are arranged. That is different from named people
   meeting and agreeing. The first is usually true and far more interesting. The second
   is usually false and always more tempting. The important exception is
   [the neoliberal thought collective](/milieus/mont-pelerin), which is a documented case
   of deliberate long-horizon coordination, and which for that reason gets the highest
   evidentiary standard on this site rather than the lowest.
4. **What a source established is not what it asserted.** Contested and discredited work
   is flagged as contested, with the name of who contests it and on what grounds.
   Wittfogel, MacLean, Le Bon, Stonor Saunders, Gatto, Milgram and Zimbardo all appear
   here with that handling.
5. Where a gap exists, the site says UNKNOWN. No invented dates, institutions, funders or
   quotations. Several milieu pages carry a funder field that says the record shows none,
   because that is what the record shows.
6. Where a section is North Atlantic by default, it says so. Most of this project is, and
   the [colonial angle](/open-questions) is its largest gap.
7. The politics are not flattened. Several figures here are politically ugly.
   [Schmitt](/thinkers/schmitt) was a Nazi. The argument gets engaged rather than avoided
   or endorsed.

## The arrangement, disclosed

This site is sequenced, and it withholds nothing about how. Arranging what a reader meets
first is choice architecture, which is a thing criticised a few pages from here, so
operating it quietly would be the site running the mechanism it documents.

### The order

The default first path is [Mills](/figures/mills), then [Han](/figures/han) or
[Deleuze](/figures/deleuze), then [Illich](/thinkers/illich), before
[Bourdieu](/thinkers/bourdieu), [Gramsci](/thinkers/gramsci) or
[Foucault](/thinkers/foucault).

The reason is what each one costs. Illich, Deleuze, Han, [Goffman](/figures/goffman) and
[Graeber](/domains/bureaucracy) describe something most people already feel and currently treat
as a personal failing, so they read as recognition. Bourdieu says the merit you were
credited with was substantially inherited. Gramsci says your opinions arrived from
somewhere. Foucault says the self doing the reading was produced. Those three are harder to
receive cold, and a reader who meets them first has no particular reason to keep going.

It is a default and not a gate. Nothing here is locked or staged.
[Start with Bourdieu](/thinkers/bourdieu) if that is what you came for.

### The pairing

No mechanism appears without its counter-practice in the same view, on the same page,
never filed somewhere else. Where the research this site is built from documents no
counter-practice, the page states that and links to the
[open questions](/open-questions) rather than going quiet or reaching for something
uplifting.

Four of the six thinker pages currently state that no counter-practice was found. That
ratio is a finding about the material and it is left visible rather than evened out.

### Why bother

A site that only documents mechanisms tends to produce readers who defend those
mechanisms, because a thing that looks inescapable gets easier to justify than to oppose.
Presenting a mechanism next to a documented instance of it being limited, contested or
refused presents it as an arrangement, which is what it is.

### What this reasoning is worth

Less than the confidence of the paragraph above it. The psychology behind these rules is
contested and sits partly inside the replication crisis. Self-affirmation effects are
smaller and more moderated than the early work claimed. The compensatory version of system
justification is argued over. The Extended Parallel Process Model comes out of health
communication, and its transfer to structural political information is an assumption
nobody here has tested.

Treat all of it as design heuristics with evidence behind them rather than as established
fact. Where checking returns one of them weaker than stated, the rule changes rather than
the wording.

## One phrase that is not used here

"Dark psychology" is a self-help label with no scholarly content behind it. The rigorous
material in [the psychology domain](/domains/psychology) is more disturbing than the
phrase suggests, and using the phrase costs credibility with exactly the readers worth
having.

The same goes for the register of "what they don't want you to know". Nothing on this
site needs it. Most of the material discussed here was published openly, proudly, by the
people who built it.
