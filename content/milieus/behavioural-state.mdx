This is the institutional history behind the [psychology domain](/domains/psychology),
and it is unusually well documented because most of it was published deliberately.

## Skinner, who said it plainly

**B. F. Skinner**, *Walden Two* (1948) and *Beyond Freedom and Dignity* (1971), argued
openly for designing environments that produce desired behaviour, and dismissed autonomy
as a pre-scientific idea. He is valuable precisely because he states without
embarrassment what later programmes do quietly. Read him to see the argument in its
undefended form.

## Tavistock

Founded in London in 1946 with initial Rockefeller funding. Group relations, human
relations in industry, applied psychoanalysis inside organizations.

It attracts an enormous amount of conspiracy literature, which is a reason to use its
own publications and serious institutional histories rather than secondary claims. The
documented version is interesting enough. The invented version is not.

## The nudge unit

The **Behavioural Insights Team** was set up inside the UK Cabinet Office in 2010, the
first state nudge unit, and dozens of governments copied the model. There is a published
trail: the MINDSPACE report (2010), the EAST framework, and randomized trials with
results in the open.

Read Thaler and Sunstein's *Nudge* (2008) as a primary document of governing technique
rather than as advice. Note the internal critique, made by sympathetic people, that
libertarian paternalism depends on the citizen not noticing the architecture. If you
notice it and object, the mechanism that was supposed to preserve your freedom to choose
turns out to have been the invisibility.

This is [Foucault's](/thinkers/foucault)

## Contemporary and algorithmic

- **Virginia Eubanks, *Automating Inequality* (2018).** Automated eligibility and risk
  scoring, tested on poor people first. The single most concrete book in this cluster.
- **Frank Pasquale, *The Black Box Society* (2015).** Authority that cannot be
  inspected.
- **Cathy O'Neil, *Weapons of Math Destruction* (2016)** and **Safiya Noble,
  *Algorithms of Oppression* (2018).**

These pair with Zuboff in the [psychology domain](/domains/psychology) and with
Deleuze's modulation in
