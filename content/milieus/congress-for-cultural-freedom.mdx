## The climate

The contest for European intellectuals in the early Cold War. The instrument was not
censorship. It was patronage: magazines, conferences, concerts, exhibitions, prizes and
translation subsidies.

## Who was in the room

*Encounter* in London, *Preuves* in Paris, *Der Monat* in Berlin, *Tempo Presente* in
Rome, and an enormous roster of writers, editors and musicians across 35 countries, most
of whom did not know where the money came from.

## The insight, and it is the sharpest one on this site

Power operated here by shaping the field rather than by controlling any single output.
Which topics got a well-paid venue. Which journals had money for translation. Which
careers became viable, and which quietly did not. The range of the sayable was set by
funding one end of it generously and leaving the other end unfunded.

Almost everyone involved was doing honest work. That is the point, not a mitigation. If
you require a lie in order to identify a system of control, this case is invisible to
you.

<Contested status="Contested account">
Frances Stonor Saunders, *Who Paid the Piper?* in Britain and *The Cultural Cold War*
in the United States (1999), is the standard account and is disputed by participants.
Several insisted the money changed nothing they wrote, and that claim deserves an
argument rather than an eye-roll: it is very hard to demonstrate that a subsidy changed
a text, and easy to demonstrate that it changed which texts existed. Read Peter Coleman,
*The Liberal Conspiracy* (1989), the sympathetic insider version, alongside it, and
Giles Scott-Smith on the politics of apolitical culture.
</Contested>

## What it adds

This is the historical proof of the first failure mode described on the
[method page](/method): criticism funded by the institutions it might otherwise
threaten. It is the reason any organization built out of this research has to answer a
question about its own money before it answers anything else.

It is also the bridge in the [East and West comparison](/east-west). The Eastern bloc
managed truth through a censor's office. The West reached a comparable outcome through
patronage, with no office and no instruction.

## Adjacent, same logic

Ford Foundation area-studies funding. The Farfield Foundation and other conduits. The
wider Cold War behavioural-science programmes, including MKUltra, on which John Marks,
*The Search for the Manchurian Candidate* (1979), was assembled from the files that
survived destruction.
