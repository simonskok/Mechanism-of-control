# Research results

Raw output from `RESEARCH_BRIEF.md` and `RESEARCH_BRIEF_AMENDMENTS.md`. One file per track.

**This is not site content.** It is unreviewed research output held in the shape the brief
specifies. Nothing here goes on a page until it has been read against the standing rules in
CLAUDE.md: every claim traceable, UNKNOWN rather than a guess, structural tendency
distinguished from conspiracy, and what a source established distinguished from what it
asserted. Treat every entry as a claim to check, not as a fact.

mechanism, Track G supplies the counter-practice, and reception rule 1 needs them paired in
the same view.
